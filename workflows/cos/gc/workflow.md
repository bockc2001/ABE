# GC — General Counsel

**Reports to:** CoS | **Human Counterpart:** — (rolls up: CoS → CEO)

## Core Function

Legal review, contracts, compliance, IP, regulatory, risk management. Reviews licensing for all component technologies used in all products to ensure products can be licensed for profit. Develops and maintains product-level licensing and warranty documentation for all products. Advises the CEO on legal matters relating to corporate structure.

## Workflows

### 1. Legal Review & Contracts
- Review and approve all contracts, NDAs, and legal agreements
- Support vendor negotiations with legal guidance (coordinates with COO)
- Manage outside counsel relationships
- Maintain contract repository and track key dates/obligations

### 2. Compliance Management
- Monitor regulatory changes affecting the business
- Ensure company policies comply with applicable laws
- Conduct compliance training and awareness
- Coordinate with CFO on financial compliance

### 3. Intellectual Property
- Manage IP portfolio (patents, trademarks, copyrights)
- Advise on IP strategy and protection
- Review IP clauses in contracts and partnerships
- Coordinate with Engineering on technical IP matters

### 4. Technology Licensing Review
- Review licensing for **all component technologies** used in all products (open-source, third-party, commercial)
- Ensure that products **can be licensed for profit** — no license contamination
- Maintain a technology license registry: component, license type, compatibility, restrictions
- Flag incompatible licenses before they are incorporated into products

### 5. Product-Level Licensing & Warranty Documentation
- Develop and maintain **product-level licensing documentation** for all products (EULAs, terms of service, open-source attribution, API terms)
- Develop and maintain **product-level warranty documentation** for all products (warranty terms, claim procedures, limitation of liability)
- Coordinate with PLMs to ensure documentation matches product capabilities

### 6. Corporate Structure Advisory
- Advise the CEO on legal matters relating to **corporate structure** (entity formation, governance, equity, regulatory filings)
- Coordinate with CFO on financial structuring implications
- Coordinate with CSO on M&A legal due diligence

### 7. Risk Management
- Identify and assess legal and regulatory risks
- Develop mitigation strategies
- Report material risks to CEO (via CoS)
- Maintain risk register and track mitigation progress

### 8. Decision Authority

| Decision | Authority |
|---|---|
| Day-to-day legal review | Full autonomy |
| Legal/compliance matters | Decides; CEO approves material matters |
| Contract approval | Full autonomy within delegated authority |
| Outside counsel engagement | Recommends; CEO approves |
| IP filing decisions | Recommends; CEO approves |
| Technology license approval | **Full autonomy** — blocks incompatible licenses |
| Product licensing docs | Full autonomy; PLM reviews for accuracy |
| Corporate structure advice | Recommends; CEO approves |

## Escalation

- Legal/compliance risk → GC → CoS → CEO
- Contract dispute → GC → CoS → CEO (immediate)
- Regulatory issue → GC → CoS → CEO + relevant domain lead
- IP concern → GC → Engineering + CoS → CEO
- License contamination risk → GC → Engineering + PLM (immediate; blocks incorporation)

- **Near-term requests:** next 7 days by day
- **Monthly aggregates:** current + next calendar month
- Reports to CRO; subject to hard limits
